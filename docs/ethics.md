# Change Coin code of ethics

Change Coin is founded on four principles: transparency, emotional intelligence, destigmatizing mental health, and perseverance.

Profit sustains the mission. Virtue defines it. Human value comes before economic value, because relationships are what actually drive people.

The purpose is to see the whole person, including their past and future selves. A generous retirement match is for the future self. Heart-centered care, including access to therapy, is for the past self. The present is empathy for how complicated people are.

Other principles that belong here: vulnerability, compassion, and the stoic set — wisdom, justice, temperance, courage.

## 1. Human first, employee second

Each person is a human before they are an employee. Hardship — mental or physical illness, grief, crisis — gets empathy, not pressure.

- Paid, protected leave when it is needed. Healing is not rushed.
- Taking care of yourself does not put the job at risk.

## 2. Transparency

Payroll and compensation are open inside the company. Secrecy makes competition. Openness makes accountability. The same rule applies to decisions and ethics.

## 3. Freedom of place

Remote-first. Work from anywhere. Optional co-op space, no required attendance.

## 4. Profit with principle

- 5% of profits go to mental-health charities.
- If ethical profit becomes impossible, close rather than compromise.

## 5. Financial well-being

Past, present, and future.

- Above-market 401(k) match.
- Ongoing personal-finance education for the team.
- Equity may be offered in a way that matches the mission. The product name is not a promise of a tradable coin.

Financial literacy and security are mental-health issues.

## 6. Regenerative thinking

Operate as a living system. Actions should nourish rather than exploit. Partnerships should sustain rather than drain. Weigh the person, the community, and the environment.

## 7. Stoic virtues in practice

- Wisdom: understand before acting.
- Courage: do what is right when it is hard.
- Justice: fairness and dignity.
- Temperance: balance and humility in success.

We will not be perfect. We will reflect and improve.

Change Coin exists to show that doing good and doing well can be the same thing.
