# Roadmap

Written for a late-2025 start. Status as of these notes: planning, not shipped.

## 2025 — get the people and the shape

- List who is actually in, and for which role: dev lead, iOS, iOS QA, business analysis, project manager, accountant, marketing, writer, tax advisor, Discord mod, life coach.
- Funding and the LLC. Shares from capital, time available, and knowledge. Putting money in puts a name on the LLC. A later C-corp is a handshake until it is not.
- Prep the work. Machines, cost estimates, and a January 2026 whiteboard so requirements are heard before they are frozen.
- App flow: account setup, home after setup, which widgets belong in phase 1.
- Architecture. UIKit is the known tool. SwiftUI is what Apple is pushing, so the lead has to get current. Decide how code is reviewed before QA, how existing iOS practice gets taught to this team, and whether pairing is the start.

## 2026 — file it and grind

- File the business.
- Learn Swift and build the app.
- Hook Discord to a Patreon-style membership. That is the nest egg.
- Decide how often the board and top-tier Discord members meet.
- Apple developer account.
- Submit for review before year-end. Expect the first submission to be rejected.
- Partner with local Ohio charities.
- Open question: can the first launch be Ohio only?

## What “done” meant in the pitch

Immediate: who is in, how equity and funding split, foundational work, core team.

2026: paperwork, Swift, paid community, Apple account, a review submission.

2027–2030: paid community, friends-and-family funding, limited ads, referral and signup partnerships, then therapist and advisor referrals.
