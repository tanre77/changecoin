# Change Coin — product

Mission: help people see their life at a glance. Unify the tools they already use so anyone who wants to get better can see clearly and act with purpose.

Audience: any American with an iPhone who wants to improve.

Tone: calm technology. No aggressive notifications, no infinite scroll, an interface that respects time and mental state. Privacy and well-being are foundational, not a later setting. People show up with avatars or pseudonyms so financial and mental-health data is not tied to a real name in the community.

The app is a bridge to resources, not a destination.

## Journey

### Level 1 — Sanctuary (stabilize)

Crisis management, debt payoff, mood tracking, a safety net.

Tools: simple budgeting, Low Power Mode, stop the bleeding.

Community: mentors and support groups.

### Level 2 — Launchpad (build)

Investing, compounding, business building, aggressive saving.

Tools: investment tracking, net worth, risk.

Community: masterminds, strategy, and the chance to become a mentor.

## Behaviors the product is built around

- Velocity check. If spending suddenly speeds up (several transactions in an hour, or one purchase over a limit), the app does not only block it. It asks for a vibe check: rate energy 1–10. A high rating can put a 24-hour hold on the transfer and can tell a trusted circle that activity looks high.
- Low Power Mode. When someone logs that they feel low, the interface simplifies, investment charts hide, and only essentials (rent, utilities) stay on autopay.
- No shame copy. A miss is “we went off track, let’s re-stabilize,” not a red overspent banner.
- Percentages over dollars in the community. “Savings rate up 10% this month” is allowed. “I made $50,000 on a trade” is not.
- Education aimed at building wealth when your brain works differently, not only “how to save.”
- A founder’s corner for the real journey.
- Community leaders are vetted. No get-rich-quick advice.

## MindCoin

Early on, skip a real cryptocurrency. Users earn MindCoins for tasks that help them. Coins redeem for gifts. They are a reward for looking after mental and physical health, not a tradable asset.

Possible coin types later: finance, mental, physical, social.

Redeem for: company shirt, gift card, flowers, planting a tree, food, books and journals, paid Discord, virtual or in-person yoga / meditation / workout, sending appreciation to a support person.

Rules:

- Cap rewards per day and week so the economy cannot be farmed.
- Rotate limited rewards.
- Bonus events (Mental Health Week: double points).
- Users should not be able to buy extra MindCoins. Buying your way through a crisis fights the point. This is debatable; it is also a possible revenue line.
- Leaderboard is badges, not dollar flexing.
- Gift coins to people you see doing well.
- Spend coins on expert chats.
- Donate coins to charity.

Open questions: coins per activity, how completion is proved, coins per dollar of reward, daily and weekly cap, and how to pay more coins for a new habit than for one someone already does every day.

Earn coins from: referrals, daily habits, streaks, milestones, encouraging other people, the email list, social follows and engagement, volunteering (proof still unsolved).

## How it could make money

- Paid Discord after ads come off the app, Patreon-style.
- A friends-and-family round, kept small.
- Ad revenue from the stores, with rules on what can be shown.
- Referral links inside personal-finance education.
- Promoting good companies by letting people earn coins, not by dumping AdSense on them.
- A podcast or channel about building the company.
- Business checking signup bonuses.
- Later: referrals to therapists, doctors, and advisors, with documentation.
- Affiliate relationships.
- Undercutting high-interest debt, with a floor tied to a high-yield savings rate.
- An assistant that can tell “talk to a human” from “walk and breathe.”
- Online funding if the friends-and-family money runs out.
